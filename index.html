How It's Made

From Tree to Pack

Four simple steps — and nothing artificial in between.

Handpicked

We source perfectly ripe, fresh fruit directly from Sri Lankan growers.

Washed & Sliced

Cleaned with filtered water and cut into even slices for a consistent crunch.

Slow Dehydration

Dried for 10–14 hours at a controlled low temperature to lock in flavour.

Sealed Fresh

Cooled and sealed in air-tight, food-grade packs, then shipped straight to you.
